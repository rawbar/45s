Privacy Policy

45s Expert Challenge (the "Game") · Last updated: November 15, 2026

45s Expert Challenge is a Forty-Fives card game that you can play on the web at www.mingya45s.com and in our Android app. It is operated by Merrimack Valley Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you register you choose a username, a 4-digit PIN and an avatar. Your PIN is verified on our server and stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read it.
  • Email address. We collect your email address so that we can send you emails about game updates. We do not use it for promotion or marketing, and we do not sell or share it.
  • Gameplay and stats. Games you play (including games against the computer), scores, wins and losses, streaks, badges earned, your theme setting, and when you last logged in.
  • Gameplay logs. For registered players, the Game records diagnostic logs of in-game decisions (such as bids, discards and cards played) tied to your username. They are recorded to improve the computer opponents' play and to find and fix bugs.
  • Voice chat (optional). In online games with other people you can use voice chat. If you allow microphone access, your voice is transmitted in real time to the other players in that game through our voice provider, Agora. We do not record or store voice audio.
  • Information stored on your device. Your user ID, username and avatar are saved in your device's local storage so you stay signed in. A service worker caches the Game's files so it loads faster.
  • Technical information. Like any web service, our hosting and infrastructure providers (see below) may receive your IP address, device and browser type, and the pages or files requested.

We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number, contact list, location or payment information.

What other players can see

Your username, avatar, online status, game count, win rate, points and badges are shown to other players in the lobby and on the leaderboard. Do not use a username that reveals personal information.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, and to let you sign in.
  • To run games, the lobby, the leaderboard, stats and badges.
  • To improve the computer opponents' play, and to fix bugs and improve the Game.
  • To email you about game updates.

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or promotion. The Game contains no ads. The service providers listed below process data only to run the Game for us.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the Game. They process data on our behalf, only to provide their service, under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Realtime Database, Cloud Functions and App Check, which uses Google reCAPTCHA to tell real app instances from bots) for accounts, game data and security.
  • Agora for real-time voice chat.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the website and app files.
  • Google Fonts and content delivery networks (such as cdnjs) that deliver fonts and code libraries, and so may see your IP address when your device loads them.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and security of the Game and its players.

Retention and deleting your data

We keep your account and gameplay data for as long as your account exists. To delete your account and the data associated with it (account, avatar, email, stats, badges and gameplay logs), email us at the address below from the email on your account, or include your username and tell us the request is for account deletion. We will delete the data within 30 days. Some data may remain in backups for a short period afterward.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We protect data with industry-standard measures, including server-side PIN verification and rate limiting of PIN attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a PIN you do not use elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
